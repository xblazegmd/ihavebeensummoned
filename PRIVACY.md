# Privacy Policy
By using this Application, you are logging in with your *Geometry Dash Account*. Therefore, you are subject to the [Geometry Dash Privacy Policy](https://www.robtopgames.com/privacy.html).

### Data sent to external sources
This Application sends data related to your *Geometry Dash Account* to the *Geometry Dash Servers*, with the objective to publish comments to the Game. The data being sent includes:
- Your *Geometry Dash username*
- Your *Geometry Dash Account ID*
- Your *Geometry Dash Account's password* (encoded in GJP2)

By having a *Geometry Dash Account*, you agreed to the [Geometry Dash Privacy Policy](https://www.robtopgames.com/privacy.html) and [Geometry Dash's Terms of Service](https://www.robtopgames.com/tos.html), therefore the use of this data is the responsibility of *RobTop Games AB*. __WE ARE NOT RESPONSIBLE FOR ANY MISUSE OF THE DATA SENT TO THE GEOMETRY DASH SERVERS__.

### Data stored locally
For keeping users logged into the Application, __your *Geometry Dash username*, and *Geometry Dash Account ID* gets stored locally in the Application's save file__, to later get sent to the *Geometry Dash Servers*. For your privacy and safety, your *Geometry Dash Account's password* does NOT get stored locally in your device, therefore you have to retype your password every time you run the Application.

### Personal information
We do NOT collect, use, or disclose any personal information from any user. Only information being used is related to the User's *Geometry Dash Account*.

### Children (ofc)
We do NOT collect, use, or disclose personal information from any individual under the age of 13. We understand the privacy and safety of these individuals is very important, due to the constant threats they have online.

As for information related to the User's *Geometry Dash Account*, we do not knowingly collect, use, or disclose information related to the User's *Geometry Dash Account* from any individual under the age of 13. According to the [Geometry Dash Terms of Service](https://www.robtopgames.com/tos.html), to even make a *Geometry Dash Account* the individual must be over the age of 13. However, we cannot know for certain if the user is over the age of 13 and correctly following the [Geometry Dash Terms of Service](https://www.robtopgames.com/tos.html) (any violation to these terms of service is responsibility of *RobTop Games AB*), so we cannot know for sure if the collected, used, or disclosed data related to the User's *Geometry Dash Account* is from an individual under the age of 13. __WE ARE NOT RESPONSIBLE FOR ANY MISUSE OF THE DATA RELATED TO THE USER'S GEOMETRY DASH ACCOUNT FROM ANY INDIVIDUAL UNDER THE AGE OF 13__
